# Multi-currency Converter

A small web app for converting between as many currencies as you like. Type an amount in any currency and the rest update instantly.

**Open it:** https://james-debono.github.io/multi-currency-converter-webapp/

- Tap **Add currency** and search by currency or country
- Press and hold a currency to move it, swipe left to remove it
- Remembers your currencies and amounts on your device
- Installs to your home screen and still opens offline, using the last rates it loaded

Rates are mid-market rates from [ExchangeRate-API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com), updated daily, with [exchange-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api) as a backup. Banks and money changers charge a margin on top.
